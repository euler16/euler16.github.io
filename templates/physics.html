Computational physics: the double pendulum

Two equal masses on rigid rods. From the Lagrangian \(L = T - V\), the Euler–Lagrange equations give coupled, non-linear equations for \(\theta_1\) and \(\theta_2\), integrated here in real time with fourth-order Runge–Kutta. Tiny changes in the starting angle produce completely different paths: chaos.

\[\ddot\theta_1 = \frac{-3g\sin\theta_1 - g\sin(\theta_1-2\theta_2) - 2\sin(\theta_1-\theta_2)\,\big(\dot\theta_2^{2}\ell + \dot\theta_1^{2}\ell\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2)\big)}{\ell\,\big(3-\cos(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)\big)}\]
\[\ddot\theta_2 = \frac{2\sin(\theta_1-\theta_2)\,\big(2\dot\theta_1^{2}\ell + 2g\cos\theta_1 + \dot\theta_2^{2}\ell\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2)\big)}{\ell\,\big(3-\cos(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)\big)}\]
