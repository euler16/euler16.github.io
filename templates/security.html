Security is a design decision, not a feature.

Since 2021 I've worked as a cyber security analyst, helping victims of cybercrime and the people investigating it. I bring the same thinking to the software I build: encrypt what is stored, collect only what is needed, and keep people in control of every sensitive step.

Areas of focus

What I spend my security time on.

Information security

Confidentiality, integrity and availability in practice: data-at-rest encryption, key management, authentication and least privilege.

Cybercrime response & forensics

Investigating incidents, identifying the people behind them, and helping victims through legal complaints.

Education & awareness

Teaching data safety clearly, without jargon or fear: phishing, passwords, account recovery and the everyday risks behind most compromises.

Security research

Studying how vulnerabilities arise in web and mobile applications, and how they are found and responsibly disclosed.

Security in my own projects

Concrete decisions from code I have shipped.

SmartLedger: encryption at rest

The business database is encrypted with SQLCipher, keys are held by the Android Keystore, the app locks behind a PIN or biometrics, and Google Drive backups are encrypted before upload.

WordSmrt: nothing to leak

No permissions, no accounts, no servers. Data minimisation as architecture: what never leaves the device cannot be intercepted or breached.

BD Railway Helper: hard boundaries

The automation never touches login, CAPTCHA, OTP or payment. Every security-sensitive step stays with the user, and the extension stores no secrets.
